## Project Overview
This project focuses on **analyzing healthcare claims data** to identify key cost drivers, trends, and patterns across different demographics and regions. The aim is to provide actionable insights to optimize healthcare spending and improve decision-making.

**Dataset:** 1,300+ healthcare claims records with information such as patient age, BMI, smoking status, region, and claim cost.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze healthcare claim costs by demographic factors like age, BMI, and smoking status.  
- Identify regions with higher or lower average claim costs.  
- Explore correlations between patient profiles and claim amounts.  
- Build interactive dashboards to visualize trends and patterns.  

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Extracted, filtered, and aggregated claims data.  
- **Excel:** Created pivot tables, summary reports, and charts.  
- **Power BI:** Built interactive dashboards and visualizations.  
- **Data Analysis & Visualization:** Identified patterns, trends, and outliers in the dataset.  

---

## Key Insights
- **Age vs. Claim Costs:** Older patients tend to have higher average claim costs.  
- **BMI Impact:** Higher BMI is correlated with higher healthcare claim amounts.  
- **Smoking Status:** Smokers have a significantly higher average claim cost than non-smokers.  
- **Regional Trends:** Certain regions show disproportionately higher claims, indicating potential areas for cost optimization.  

---

## Dashboards & Visualizations
1. **Claim Cost by Age Group:** Bar charts showing average claim amounts across age ranges.  
2. **BMI vs. Claim Cost:** Scatter plots highlighting the correlation between BMI and costs.  
3. **Smoking Status Analysis:** Pie chart and bar chart comparing smokers and non-smokers.  
4. **Regional Analysis:** Map visualization of average claim costs by region.

   

---

## Dataset
The dataset contains the following fields:
- `Patient_ID`: Unique identifier for each patient  
- `Age`: Age of the patient  
- `BMI`: Body Mass Index  
- `Smoking_Status`: Smoker or Non-Smoker  
- `Region`: Patient’s location  
- `Claim_Amount`: Total healthcare claim  

**Source:** [CMS Healthcare Data](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/search?theme=Hospitals)
